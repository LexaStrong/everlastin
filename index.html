Our 11 Specialized Services

Engineered solutions for every critical vulnerability in your building envelope.

01

Roof Waterproofing

Heavy-duty bituminous and liquid-applied elastomeric coatings that resist standing water, blistering tropical heat, and heavy downpours.

  • UV-resistant reflective membranes
  • Flat and pitched concrete roofs

02

Basement Waterproofing

Positive and negative side hydrostatic tanking to halt water penetration, mold growth, and high water-table seepage permanently.

  • Internal pressure tanking
  • Subterranean damp barrier
WhatsApp Enquiry for Basement
03

Foundation Protection

Essential footings and foundation sealants preventing capillary action (rising damp) from entering ground-floor masonry walls.

  • Damp-proof course (DPC) installation
  • Chemical soil-side membrane
WhatsApp Enquiry for Foundation
04

Concrete Waterproofing

Crystalline admixtures and surface impregnators that chemically react with unhydrated cement to seal micro-cracks and capillaries.

  • Self-healing crystalline technology
  • Rebar rust prevention
WhatsApp Enquiry for Concrete
05

Balcony Waterproofing

Under-tile and exposed polyurethane systems stopping rainwater from migrating into living spaces and deteriorating structural beams below.

  • Flexible thermal movement joints
  • Non-slip tile underlayment
WhatsApp Enquiry for Balcony
06

Water Tank Sealing

Certified food-grade and potable water approved polymer coatings for concrete overhead and underground water reservoirs.

  • Non-toxic, drinking water certified
  • Algae and pressure resistant

Retaining Wall Protection

Heavy-duty earth-retaining barriers combined with geocomposite drainage boards to divert earth pressure and hydrostatic load.

  • Root penetration resistance
  • Pressure relief drainage systems

Leakage Repairs & Injection

High-pressure polyurethane chemical injection grouting to stop active water gushing in cracks, joints, and pipe penetrations within minutes.

  • Instant active leak stoppage
  • No destructive demolition required

Terrace Waterproofing

Swimming Pool Waterproofing
